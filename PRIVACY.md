# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** June 14, 2026

Marble ("the app," "we," "us") is a personal training journal. This
policy explains what the app collects, how it is used, and the choices
you have. We've kept it short and plain because the app collects very
little.

## The short version

Marble stores your training data so you can keep a record of your
workouts. Your data is yours. We do not sell it, share it with
advertisers, or use it for advertising. You can delete it at any time.

## What the app collects

**Account information (optional).** If you choose to create an account
or sign in, we collect the email address and name associated with that
sign-in. You can use the app without an account by choosing "Skip for
now" during setup — in that case no account information is collected and
your data stays on your device.

**Training data.** The workouts, programs, exercises, sets, weights,
reps, bodyweight entries, and notes you enter. This is the core content
of the app.

**Progress photos (optional).** If you choose to attach a photo to a
workout, that image is stored. The app requests camera and photo
library access only when you actively add a photo, and only the photos
you select are used.

## How your data is stored

Your data is stored locally on your device. If you sign in to an
account, your data is also stored in our cloud backend (Google Firebase:
Firestore for training data and Firebase Storage for photos) so it can
sync across your devices and be restored if you reinstall the app.
Cloud storage is provided by Google; their handling of data is governed
by Google's privacy practices.

If you do not sign in, your data never leaves your device.

## How your data is used

Your data is used solely to provide the app's features: recording and
displaying your training history, syncing across your devices, and
restoring your record if you reinstall. We do not use your data for
advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to running the app, and
we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

## Your choices and controls

- **Use without an account.** Choose "Skip for now" to keep all data on
  your device only.
- **Delete individual items.** Workouts, programs, and photos can be
  deleted within the app at any time.
- **Clear all data.** Settings includes a "Clear all data" action that
  removes your local data.
- **Delete your account.** If you have an account, Settings includes a
  "Delete account" action that removes your account and associated
  profile.

## Children

Marble is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly
collect information from them.
