# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** July 9, 2026

Marble ("the app," "we," "us") is a personal training journal. This
policy explains what the app collects, how it's used, and the choices
you have. We've kept it short and plain because the app collects little
and does simple things with it.

## The short version

Marble stores your training data so you can keep a record of your
workouts and access it across your devices. Your data is yours. We do
not sell it, share it with advertisers, or use it for advertising or
tracking. You can delete it at any time.

## What we collect

Marble requires an account, so your data is tied to your account and
backed up so it isn't lost.

- **Account information.** When you sign in with Apple or with an email
  and password, we collect your email address and the name on your
  profile.
- **Training data.** The workouts, templates, exercises, sets, weights,
  reps, bodyweight entries, and notes you enter — the core content of
  the app.
- **Progress photos.** If you attach a photo to a workout or set a
  profile picture, that image is stored. The app requests camera and
  photo-library access only when you actively add a photo, and only the
  photos you choose are used.
- **Account identifier.** A unique ID for your account, used to keep
  your data associated with you and synced across your devices.

We do **not** collect location, contacts, usage analytics, advertising
identifiers, or diagnostic data, and Marble contains no advertising or
tracking SDKs.

## How your data is stored

Your data is stored on your device and in our cloud backend so it can
sync across your devices and be restored if you reinstall the app. The
cloud backend is Google Firebase — Firebase Authentication for sign-in,
Cloud Firestore for training data, and Firebase Storage for photos.
Sign in with Apple is provided by Apple. Their handling of data is
governed by Google's and Apple's respective privacy practices.

Your data is stored under your account and is not accessible to other
users.

## How your data is used

Your data is used solely to run the app: recording and displaying your
training history, syncing across your devices, and restoring your record
if you reinstall. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling,
tracking across apps or websites, or any purpose unrelated to running
the app, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

## Your choices and controls

- **Delete individual items.** Workouts, templates, and photos can be
  deleted within the app at any time.
- **Delete your account.** Settings includes a "Delete account" action
  that permanently deletes your account, profile, and the training data
  and photos stored in the cloud under your account.

## Children

Marble is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly
collect information from them.
